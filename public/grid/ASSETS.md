# Grid asset provenance

The Grid bundles the following Poly Haven assets locally. Poly Haven publishes
these assets under CC0 1.0, so attribution is not required; the links are kept
here for provenance and reproducible updates.

- `environment/modern_buildings_night_1k.hdr`
  - Modern Buildings Night HDRI
  - https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_buildings_night

License: https://polyhaven.com/license

- `materials/asphalt/{normal,roughness}.webp`: Asphalt 02, 1K OpenGL normal and roughness, lossless WebP conversion. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02
- `materials/concrete/{normal,roughness}.webp`: Concrete Floor 02, 1K OpenGL normal and roughness, lossless WebP conversion. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02
- Texture source files resolved through https://api.polyhaven.com/files/asphalt_02 and https://api.polyhaven.com/files/concrete_floor_02. Both sets CC0 1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license
