Daily support for Parkinson’s

ParkiWell

An app for managing everyday life with Parkinson’s, with symptom tracking, medication reminders, and guided speech and movement practice.

SUPPORTED EXERCISES10
BODY POINTS TRACKED33
CAMERA UPLOADS0

Role

Design and engineering

Challenge

What had to be solved

Symptoms, medication schedules, and regular practice can be difficult to keep track of. ParkiWell brings those routines together in an app designed to stay usable when tapping is difficult, movement is slower, or the connection drops.

Approach

How I built it

  1. Designed symptom logs, dose reminders, weekly practice plans, and an optional community around everyday use, with large controls, screen-reader support, and protection against accidental repeat taps.
  2. Built a motion coach that uses MediaPipe to follow 33 body points through the phone camera. A custom Dart engine counts reps and gives movement feedback through text, speech, and vibration, while keeping camera footage on the device.
  3. Stored health records locally with AES-256 encryption so tracking works offline. Changes sync to Supabase when a connection returns, with database access rules that keep each account’s private records separate.

Outcomes

What changed

  • Daily symptom and dose tracking that works offline
  • Motion coach in development with ten seated and standing exercises
  • Camera processing on the phone, with no video uploads
  • Coming to iPhone and Android
